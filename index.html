Yo. Made this website for a few reasons, the biggest being that, well, I had two hours to kill. I'm going to try to keep this as something of a central repository for my art. You probably don't know like 95% of what I do. I honestly can't tell if I make it difficult to find all my stuff on purpose. I think the answer is yes? Anyways, my purposes with this blog are such:
  1. Make a place to find all of my art
  2. Have a slightly more private place to talk longform
  3. Use social media less
Not necessarily in that order, I guess. Also, it's been years since I taught myself HTML to make my first neocities, and I've forgotten all I learned. So, I'm just pulling stuff from the internet. No shame in it, I guess, but this theme doesn't have other layouts/formats for like, blog posts and stuff, and as someone who's very aesthetically minded, I really don't want two clashing pages. I'll see what I can do.
