six corners
Six Corners is a story that takes place in the distant future where most of the world has frozen over, rendering only Earth's coastlines inhabitable. This false ice age led to the end of a single unified civilisation. Humanity now survives in tiny pockets of life, completely separated by perpetual storms that prevent any form of communication. Despite that, recent years have seen many of humanity's previous technological advancements have been recreated, and even built upon.
Eve is a surgeon in the Cascadian enclave, one of the last of the classically trained. Advancements in neural implants mean that anybody can download her skills for the price of a few gigabytes, but that's the least of her worries. When she catches a glimpse of her estranged twin for the first time in fifteen years, she happens upon a freshly dismembered body.
Confronted with reminders of their grim past, Eve must track down her sister in an attempt to clear her name.

For now, I'm planning on uploading Six Corners chapter-by-chapter on this website. It would be nice to make a bit of money off my work, so I'm considering something like a patreon for early release chapters, or maybe some behind the scenes looks at my writing process. Maybe scans of my handwritten first drafts? I really have no idea. I also considered just hosting the first few chapters here, and having the full thing available for a few bucks on itch, or another similar host. (I also might sell physical copies in addition to one of those two options, but they'd be quite expensive. I'm not going to a boutique publisher, and hand-crafting them takes quite a lot of time and money).
It would be nice to hear what other people think about this. No comment section here, unfortunately, so maybe reply to my bluesky post about this topic, or email me (j@jartist.gay). Would you prefer a weekly release, or a full novel all at once? Would you be interested in a patreon for a few bucks a month to get chapters a week early? Or just to support me in general?
There's definitely pros and cons to each option, but I'm definitely biased towards a weekly release - I just love serialised art. In my opinion, very little beats getting to experience a show or comic or book week by week. And hosting everything here means that I get to do fun things like play with font, colors, formatting, etc. I'm still pretty early in the actual writing process, though, so I have a lot of time to mull everything over.
On top of that, I'm going to be very very busy come July, so who knows if I'm going to be able to finish it at all, let alone in a timely manner. I have a serious problem with promising and not delivering with regards to art, as I tend to just lose steam on big projects, so I'm not going to start publishing until I have like, 90% of it written. Ideally, I don't release anything until it's 100% finished and I'm 100% satisfied, but I love what I make and I want to share it with other people, so we'll see when my will starts to crumble.

Quick aside: you may wonder why I'm not going to release my first book, Paracosm, in this way. It's already completely written, after all. Well, there's more than a few reasons. The biggest is that 3DS is my dream story, and if I'm ever getting anything of mine traditionally published, then I really want it to be that, and tradpub isn't very big on re-releasing any previously available material (unless it's an enormous hit). Another reason is that since I finished my last edit, I've been thinking a lot about it, and I would want another heavy editing pass before I'd be comfortable releasing it to the public. Last of reasons I want to litigate here is that Paracosm is only one very small microcosm (heh) of the story I want to tell, and I only want to put my work out there if I'm actually going to deliver. (And because I want to be able to make any needed continuity/foreshadowing edits to Paracosm before anyone else sees it, and that's only possible if I'm basically done with the sequel.)
(That being said, I'm totally down to have people beta read it. If you're interested, let me know, and give me like a month (probably way more) to do an edit.)
