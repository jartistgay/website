six corners
Six Corners is a story that takes place in the distant future where most of the world has frozen over, rendering only Earth's coastlines inhabitable. This false ice age led to the end of a single unified civilisation. Humanity now survives on islands, in enclaves, completely separated by the impenetrable ice. Despite that, many of humanity's previous technological advancements have been recreated, and even built upon.
Eve is a surgeon in the Cascadian enclave, one of the last of the classically trained. Advancements in neural implants mean that anybody can download her skills for the price of a few gigabytes. But that's the least of her worries. When she catches a glimpse of her estranged twin for the first time in fifteen years,

For now, I'm planning on uploading Six Corners chapter-by-chapter on this website. It would be nice to make a few bucks off my work, so I'm considering something like a patreon for early release chapters, or maybe some behind the scenes looks at my writing process. I really have no idea. I also considered just hosting the first few chapters here, and having the full thing available for a few bucks on itch, or another similar host. (I also might sell physical copies, but they'd be quite expensive. I'm not going to a boutique publisher, and hand-crafting them takes quite a lot of time and money).
It would be nice to hear what other people think about this (weekly vs batch release). No comment section here, so maybe reply to my bluesky post about this topic, or email me (j@jartist.gay).
There's definitely pros and cons to each option, but I'm definitely biased towards a weekly release - I just love serialised art. In my opinion, very little beats getting to experience a show or comic or book week by week. And hosting everything here means that I get to do fun things like play with font, colors, formatting, etc. I'm still pretty early in the process, though, so I have a lot of time to mull everything over.
Plus, I'm going to be very very busy come July, so who knows if I'm going to be able to finish it. I have a serious problem with promising and not delivering WRT art, as I tend to just lose steam on big projects, so I'm not going to start publishing until I have like, 90% of it written, ideally 90% edited.
