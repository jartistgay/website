chapter 2
I woke up in a hospital bed. No, not a real hospital, like the ones I would come to know, but even though I was fifteen, I had never been to a hospital before. I had only read about them in books, and this was close enough that I assumed it was where I had to be. My recent memory was fragmented, and it wasn’t for a long while until I picked up all the pieces. But I saw my sister sleeping next to me, in one of those archetypical bedside chairs, and the idea thrilled me. I was a romantic back then, and the idea of waking to a scene out of a story - well. For a while I thought I was in a story. I think that was leftover from the opiates. Back then, I’m not sure I even knew what drugs were, that they altered your mental state. Father drank, but rarely, and I never saw him drunk, per se. Although it was probably difficult to tell, with his episodes. So when I say that I woke up in a place I had never been before, not knowing how I had gotten there, with a lightness and euphoria I hadn’t thought possible, my parents nowhere in sight, you must understand me when I say that I believed my feverish prayers had been answered and I had awoken inside one of my favourite novels, ready to embark on a grand adventure.
