six corners
Six Corners is a story that takes place in a distant future where most of the world has frozen over, rendering only Earth's coastlines inhabitable. This false ice age led to the end of a single unified civilisation. Humanity now survives in tiny pockets of life, completely separated by perpetual storms that prevent any form of communication. Despite that, recent years have seen many of humanity's previous technological creations have been reinvented, and even built upon.
Eve lives in the Cascadian enclave, one of the last of the classically trained surgeons. Advancements in neural implants mean that nowadays, anybody can download her skills for the price of a few gigabytes, but that's the least of her worries. When she catches a glimpse of her estranged twin for the first time in fifteen years, she follows her, only to happen upon a freshly dismembered body.
Confronted with reminders of their past, Eve must track down her sister in an attempt to clear her name.

For now, I'm planning on uploading Six Corners chapter-by-chapter on this website. It would be nice to make a bit of money off my work, so I'm considering something like a patreon for early release chapters or behind the scenes looks at my writing process. Maybe scans of my handwritten first drafts? I really have no idea. I also considered just hosting the first few chapters here, and having the full thing available for a few bucks on itch, or another similar host. (I also may sell physical copies in addition to one of those two options, but they'd be quite expensive. I'm not going to a boutique publisher, and hand-crafting them takes quite a lot of time and money.)
It would be nice to hear what other people think about this. No comment section here, unfortunately, so maybe reply to my bluesky post about this topic, or email me (j@jartist.gay). Would you prefer a weekly release, or a full novel all at once? Would you be interested in a patreon for a few bucks a month to get chapters a week early? Or just to support me in general?
There's definitely pros and cons to each option, but I'm biased towards a weekly release - I just love serialised art. In my opinion, very little beats getting to experience a show or comic or book week by week, and hosting everything here means that I get to do fun things like play with font, colors, formatting, etc. I'm still pretty early in the actual writing process, though, so I have a lot of time to mull everything over. Your feedback is appreciated!
