October 24, 1949
Gimme Shelter
War, children - it's just a shot away.

“This is a National Emergency Message requested by the White House. All broadcast stations will follow activation procedures in the CONELRAD Operating Handbook for a national level emergency. The President of the United States or his representative will shortly deliver a message over CONELRAD.
“We interrupt our programming; this is a national emergency. Important instructions will follow.
“This is a National Emergency Message. All broadcast stations and cable systems shall transmit this National Emergency Message. This station has interrupted its regular programming at the request of the White House to participate in CONELRAD. During this emergency, most stations will remain on the air providing news and information to the public in assigned areas. This is KAGI. We will continue to serve the Grants Pass area. If you are not in this Local Area, you should tune to stations providing news and information for your Local Area. You are listening to CONELRAD serving the Grants Pass area. Do not use your telephone. The telephone lines should be kept open for emergency use. The Emergency Alert System has been activated.
“We interrupt our programming at the request of the White House. This is CONELRAD. All normal programming has been discontinued during this emergency. This is KAGI. This station will continue furnishing news, official information and instructions for the Grants Pass area. If you are not in the Grants Pass area, you should tune to stations providing news and information for your area. It is important that you listen carefully to announcements from the station in your local area.
“The following message is transmitted at the request of the United States government. This is not a drill. I repeat, this is not a drill.
“Please stand by.”
“My fellow Americans. I speak to you tonight under the gravest of circumstances. Within the last several minutes, the early detection radar systems operated by the War Department have confirmed that long-range Soviet aircraft carrying atomic-powered bombs have penetrated the eastern seaboard. Two enemy bombers remain at large.
“Military authorities believe their intended targets are the District of Columbia and the city of New York. Interceptors have been dispatched. Anti-aircraft defenses are fully engaged. Every effort is being made to destroy these aircraft before they reach their objectives.
“I pray to Almighty God that these efforts succeed.
“To every citizen within the District of Columbia and the greater New York region: Take shelter immediately. Do not delay. Do not gather your belongings. Do not attempt to leave by automobile. Proceed at once to the nearest basement, subway station, civil defense shelter, or underground structure available to you. If none are available, seek the lowest floor of a large building, away from windows and exterior walls. Drop flat on the floor, and cover your head with your arms. After the blast, do not leave until you are certain the danger has passed.
“To Americans elsewhere: remain calm. Keep your radios on. Telephone lines must remain open for military and emergency communications. Do not spread rumors. Await official instructions.
“The Soviets have carried the war to the American homeland. For a decade now, our soldiers have fought to prevent the war in Europe and Asia from touching American soil. They have done the unthinkable.
“Let there be no misunderstanding: The United States will endure. Whatever happens in the coming hour, America will continue to fight.
"To those who find themselves in danger tonight: your nation has not abandoned you. Every soldier, every pilot, every sailor, every policeman, every firefighter, every doctor, and every volunteer who can answer the call is already doing so. You are not alone.
“To the men flying against the enemy at this very moment: the hopes of an entire nation fly with you.
“Should these weapons reach our shores, history will forever remember this night as one of infamy. The night that these weapons have left the pages of science fiction and have entered reality.
“If the Soviets seek to break our spirit, they shall discover that the American people cannot be frightened into surrender. May God protect our nation. May He watch over those now in harm’s way. May He grant victory to the cause of freedom. Thank you.”
