(Manifest)
(Manifest) is a story ranging from the year 1950CE to 1107NC.
It is the story of the end of the world, its twelve lone survivors, and the worlds they build from the ashes.

This has been percolating in my mind for years; I caught glimpses of it a long, long time ago, but the first appearances of characters named "Ardana" and "Roswell" date to late 2019. By then, I had a rough idea of their story, an idea that's remained largely unchanged since.
But, as with all writing, the writing is the hardest part. You have an idea, and a feeling, but as it turns out, maybe it doesn't make as good of a story as you thought. Or on paper, there's a logistical knot you've tied yourself in that didn't exist in your mind. But "finished" is better than "perfect", and as much as I've thought about all of this, I'd rather have it be out there
