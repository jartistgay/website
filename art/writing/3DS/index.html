intro
3 Dimensional Swans (3DS for short) is a story I first dreamt up in 2017 after watching Hunter X Hunter (2011) for the first time, and have been continually thinking about for the last decade.
setting
The setting of 3DS was engineered to allow me to tell almost any type of story I desired, so any blanket statement will be overgeneralising. It has both magic and technology beyond our own, although some cultures choose to eschew one, the other, or both. Only one third of people have the ability to use magic directly, but everyone can use other, "lesser" forms of magic. Space travel is relatively common, with humanity being spread out among thousands of settlements on different planets/moons/asteroids/space stations/etc.
history
The world of 3DS is an alt-history that diverges from our own world in the early 1900s, continuing for a few thousand years until the "present day", year 1347.
(manifest)
(manifest) is a story I'm currently writing, delving into the origin and history of this world. It follows the birth of the Gods and their attempts to revive humanity.
paracosm
Paracosm is a novel I wrote in this universe, covering six years in the lives of five people on the floating island of Anima.
paracosm 2
I immediately dove into a sequel, and then realised (after writing like 20k words) that it was just too weird and confusing, and that I wanted to work on some other projects. Soon, I'm hoping to do some workshopping with a friend of mine, and at least straighten out my ideas, if not get started on it again.

dubs
This is Dubs, one of the Gods of 3DS. Gods in this world are not the people who created it, but after the extinction of all life on earth, they were the ones who invented the technology needed to re-seed humanity across the galaxy.

This piece was the first I ever did in this style. I had experimented a lot with pixel art in the past, but everything was with a much smaller pixel size and wasn't lineless. Then, for this one, I decided I wanted to work with a bigger canvas, so I could make the wallpaper work out, and when I was almost done, I just... hid the line layer, and realised how much better it looked like this. You can see even back then I was averse to drawing faces. It's still technically unfinished, and I do want to return to it someday, or at least redraw it.







paracosm
This is a cover I drew for Paracosm in a fit of sleepless mania. I had just gotten feedback from one of my beta readers when someone requested a copy of the book (I hand-craft them sometimes). Their birthday was in three weeks, and I considered that deadline reasonable for a full edit.

At some point in the process, though, I had gotten it into my head that I should draw a new cover for it. I liked the old one (courtesy of a wonderful friend of mine, she did an amazing job), but for some reason I can't remember, I really wanted to give this person a version with my own art on the front. Unfortunately, I came up closer on the deadline than I thought, and I actually had to show up late just so I could finish binding. I've cleaned it up a bit since then and now it's maybe the most proud I am of any of my drawings.

In the future, I hope to get a whole bunch of my friends to draw this cover, since memory and variations in perspective is one of the biggest themes of the novel, but for now, there's only the two versions.



after hours
These next three were all for an animatic I was planning to the song 'After Hours' by The Velvet Underground. I'm really proud of them all, the last one in particular, as they were all created with the philosophy of 'eh, fuck it', and I decided that, actually, I liked my art way more when I deliberately cast off my perfectionism. That's kind of been the philosophy I've carried forwards into art, post 2023. Not that I shouldn't care about my art, but that worrying about it wastes valuable time that can be spent actually fucking doing it.
