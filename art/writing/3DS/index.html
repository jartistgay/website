Intro
Note: This page is currently under construction. I'm still trying to clean it up and add more information where needed, but everything here is accurate, so feel free to give it a look.

3 Dimensional Swans (3DS for short) is a story I first dreamt up in 2017 after watching Hunter X Hunter (2011) for the first time, and have been continually thinking about for the last decade.
Setting
The setting of 3DS was engineered to allow me to tell almost any type of story I desired, so any blanket statement will be overgeneralising. It has both magic and technology beyond our own, although some cultures choose to eschew one, the other, or both. Only one third of people have the ability to use magic directly, but everyone can use other, "lesser" forms of magic. Space travel is relatively common, with humanity being spread out among thousands of settlements on different planets/moons/asteroids/space stations/etc.
History
The world of 3DS is an alt-history that diverges from our own world in the early 1900s, continuing for a few thousand years until the "present day", year 1347.
Content Warnings
Violence in this world is often treated quite flip. Expect blood and death and at times more serious maiming, but no hefty descriptions of gore (outside describing what a particularly nasty injury might feel like). Someone's head might get exploded, but it's going to be written as "Their head exploded". This isn't a horror story, or one that lingers on the grotesque.

The tone can get pretty bleak and melancholic at times, maybe downright depressing for some, but never expect anything truly worse than a bittersweet ending. Bad things can and will happen to our main characters, sometimes for long stretches. Often deals with topics of mental health, personality disorders, the end of the world, and religiosity.

I won't say a specific number in terms of age rating. I read works far more adult than this one when I was still in single digits, and consider myself better off because of it. If you want to read it, I encourage you to give it a shot. But do know that it is just a story, and it's not for everyone.
Schedule?
Depends on what's being published. Anything shorter than 10k will probably be posted all at once. Anything nearing 10k or longer will be published in weekly chapters every Monday. Anything nearing 80k will be published twice a week, on Mondays and Thursdays.

In between will, of course, be hiatuses. I can't write and edit a few thousand words a week, no matter how much I wish I could. Projected schedules (and word counts) are only projected, but if the first chapter of something has already released, assume that it's going to publish regularly until it's finished.

Bonus chapters will be published on the seventh of every month, unless it falls on a Monday (or scheduled Thursday). Sometimes this will be just getting another chapter early. Sometimes it will be an unrelated side story. Sometimes it will be a less plot heavy, more lighthearted story that takes place around the same time as whatever's being published. Maybe reader requests? Or a Q&A? It's really up to me. And you, if you email me.

Tubular Logic
Tubular Logic is the only story listed here that's available publically; this is because it's short, and sweet, and doesn't particularly spoil or tie into any major events in 3DS. I do still recommend reading it, and not just because I'm proud, although I most certainly am, but I feel it's a good coda for the story as a whole, a fun thematic primer for a lot of the ideas I wrestle with and the ways I tend to tackle those ideas.
(Manifest)
(Manifest) is a story I'm currently writing, delving into the origin and history of this world. It follows the birth of the Gods and their attempts to revive humanity. It's planned to be about 50k words, but who knows how long it'll be by the time I'm done with writing and edits.
Everything Must Go!
Everything Must Go! is a story that serves as something of an introduction to a location critical to this tale: the floating island of Anima. It's also planned to be about 50k words, but might end up shorter. I've written the opening and gotten a good feel for the characters, but not much beyond that.
PARACOSM
PARACOSM is a novel I wrote in this universe, covering six years in the lives of five people on Anima. This was the first thing I've ever wrote that ended up being more than a few pages; it's honestly very surprising I ended up finishing the thing (at a hefty 100k words, to boot!). But it is, of course, the first thing I ever really wrote, so it's definitely quite weak in parts, and having written more of this world and its history, I feel there's a lot of changes I'd like to make. Eventually, I'm planning a complete rewrite, with certain elements being expanded upon. I expect it to run closer to 150k when I'm done.
PARACOSM 2
After finishing PARACOSM, I immediately dove into a sequel, and then realised (after writing about 20k words) that it was just too ambitious for my skill level at the time, and that I wanted to work on some other projects. Now that I've had a decent bit of time to stew on it, I'm really itching to get here. Most of what I wrote the first go around ended up getting binned, just because the characters and setup are a lot different. I have a pretty loose outline for my ideas, but I want to turn them into something far more concrete before I really get started. It's still quite ambitious, but maybe a little less so here or there. We'll really see when I finish (Manifest) and EMG.

The only things that haven't changed are the initial premise (plot/location), and the idea that this was to serve as a crash course in the wider world of 3DS, given that every story before this is married to their own small, intimate casts and settings. Oh, and the target word count being 500k. It's essentially going to be five novels all together; you can think of (M), EMG!, and PARACOSM as essentially an introductory arc: here's what you need to know about this world, its history, its people. Here's the big players and big concepts, all playing out on a micro scale. And just when you think you get it, here comes the next arc, where everything is so much bigger and more complicated than you could ever imagine. (Of course, both these arcs are just in Part 1; I have warned you that this story will be very long.)

Oh, and the full rewrite of PARACOSM might take place closer to when I'm done with P2 just for the purposes of foreshadowing and consistency.

dubs
This is Dubs, one of the Gods of 3DS. Gods in this world are not the people who created it, but after the extinction of all life on earth, they were the ones who invented the technology needed to re-seed humanity across the galaxy.

This piece was the first I ever did in this style. I had experimented a lot with pixel art in the past, but everything was with a much smaller pixel size and wasn't lineless. Then, for this one, I decided I wanted to work with a bigger canvas, so I could make the wallpaper work out, and when I was almost done, I just... hid the line layer, and realised how much better it looked like this. You can see even back then I was averse to drawing faces. It's still technically unfinished, and I do want to return to it someday, or at least redraw it.







paracosm
This is a cover I drew for Paracosm in a fit of sleepless mania. I had just gotten feedback from one of my beta readers when someone requested a copy of the book (I hand-craft them sometimes). Their birthday was in three weeks, and I considered that deadline reasonable for a full edit.

At some point in the process, though, I had gotten it into my head that I should draw a new cover for it. I liked the old one (courtesy of a wonderful friend of mine, she did an amazing job), but for some reason I can't remember, I really wanted to give this person a version with my own art on the front. Unfortunately, I came up closer on the deadline than I thought, and I actually had to show up late just so I could finish binding. I've cleaned it up a bit since then and now it's maybe the most proud I am of any of my drawings.

In the future, I hope to get a whole bunch of my friends to draw this cover, since memory and variations in perspective is one of the biggest themes of the novel, but for now, there's only the two versions.



after hours
These next three were all for an animatic I was planning to the song 'After Hours' by The Velvet Underground. I'm really proud of them all, the last one in particular, as they were all created with the philosophy of 'eh, fuck it', and I decided that, actually, I liked my art way more when I deliberately cast off my perfectionism. That's kind of been the philosophy I've carried forwards into art, post 2023. Not that I shouldn't care about my art, but that worrying about it wastes valuable time that can be spent actually fucking doing it.
