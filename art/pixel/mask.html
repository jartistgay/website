overview

Mask was done as a challenge to myself: one page a day, every day. It was a lot of fun, and definitely helped me improve at my art. Unfortunately, while I do have storyboards for the rest of the chapter, I don't have any immediate plans to continue working on this story.

Mask is read right to left, top to bottom. Alt text is provided for clarity. As always, click for a pixel-perfect image host.
0.
A pixel art comic rendered in grey and black. There are four panels of a pair of eyes slowly widening, the panels growing in size as the eyes open more.
1.
A woman lies on a hill, arms behind her head, messy hair about her. She is wearing a round mask with eyeholes, and there is a question mark where the mouth would be. In the distance someone walks towards her. Behind the curve of the hill is a house and a cloudy sky. Across the span of the hill is the word "mask" in all caps, separating the woman from the rest of the image.
2.
a dark haired woman walks up to the woman lying on the hill.

Panel 2: Through the eyeholes of the mask, we see the dark haired woman say, "Beatrice! You're awake already. I half thought I'd be pulling you out of bed in an hour."

Panel 3: Beatrice looks down. She says "oh... um..."

Panel 4: The dark haired woman says "... Are you done packing?"

Panel 5: Beatrice looks down and says nothing. Her messy blonde hair takes up most of the panel. The question mark over the mouth of her mask has been replaced by a large bold X.

Panel 6: Beatrice looks up at the other woman, who looks down at her.

Panel 7: We see a field of flowers. The woman says "You can just say no... I'm not judging."

Panel 8: We see the woman from below, as if looking up at her. Beatrice says, "no. i havent"

Panel 10: Hand in hand, Beatrice and the woman walk across the hillside.
3.
The dark haired woman is walking down a street. Beatrice is next to her, but they are separated by a panel boarder. She says, "So... how's your dad?"

Panel 2: Beatrice says nothing, the mouth of her mask now an ellipses.

Panel 3: The dark haired woman, in profile, looks down. There is a cloud behind her that slowly drifts out of view.

Panel 4: She looks up and turns to Beatrice slightly. She says, "Y'know, I was kinda hoping we could use this to talk more."

Panel 5: She turns away again, and says "Like when we were kids."

Panel 6: She looks down again, pain in her eyes. She says "... Is that stupid?"

Panel 7: Beatrice looks up at a clear sky, head turned slightly towards the dark haired woman. She says "...no." Her mouth is blank.

4.
The dark haired woman says "Oh." She is a little surprised.

Panel 2: She walks next to Beatrice and says, "That's now what I was expecting."

Panel 3: The same image, and not the mouth on Beatrice's mask is a question mark.

Panel 4: The dark haired woman scratches her head. She says, "Since you don't like talking, I guess I can talk for the both of us."

Panel 5: Not looking at her, Beatrice nods, her mouth a check mark.

Panel 6: They arrive at an old two story house. The woman says, "Oh. We're here!"
5.
Beatrice and the dark haired woman stand silhouetted in the doorway, facing a man in a wheelchair, back to them. He is working at a table. Bright light streams from behind them, casting him in inverted tones. The woman says, "Hello, Slater! How are you?"

Panel 2: Closeup of Slater's face. He is an old man with small glasses, hunched over his table. He says, "Better now, thanks to your father. How are you, Magdalene?"

Panel 3: Magdalene, the dark haired woman, says "I'm well, thank you. I'll send your words along." Slater from offscreen says, "Mm. Could you close the door, please?"

Page 4: Closeup of Magdalene's hand and the doorknob. She says, "Of course, sir," as the door clicks shut.
6.
A closeup of Slater's work table, filled with cobbler's tools, and a single candle burning in a holder. Magdalene says, "It's a little dark- sir." Slater replies, "I like it this way. It helps me concentrate."

Panel 2: Slater turns to face Magdalene and Beatrice. Magdalene says, "I see. Um, I just came to help Beatrice pack."

Panel 3: Slater turns back to his work, glancing over his shoulder a bit. He says, "Well, don't let me hold you up. And, Magdalene?"

Panel 4: Magdalene says, "Yes?" Slater, who has turned away from them, says "Take care of my daughter." Magdalene says, "I will, sir."

Panel 5: Magdalene grabs Beatrice's hand, and the two walk away. On Beatrice's mask is an exclamation mark and a symbol of a hand. Magdalene looks slightly angry.

7.
Magdalene and Beatrice disappear up a staircase. Only the edge of Beatrice's hair is seen.
