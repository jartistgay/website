piora
Piora is an alt-history world that diverges from our own in the early 1900s, ending with humanity's extinction in the year 2016. The only survivors, the twelve Gods, were left to pick up the pieces. Fifteen hundred years later, it seems they've done a decent job, all considered. But beyond their comprehension lies enormous powers they didn't even know they were using.

josephine county blues
Josephine County Blues was a slice of life/rural fantasy TTRPG campaign inspired by Twin Peaks and Gravity Falls. It is the prequel story to 3DS, and the genesis of the world of Piora. It takes place in the sleepy mountain town of Selma, Oregon, in the year 1991, and features four people chosen by the mysterious god residing at its centre.
