WELCOME TO THE MANIFESTO!
I'm committing to a schedule. Oops! This is a horrible idea! I'm busier than I've ever been! And bad at deadlines! But I'm doing one anyway! I will be writing more 3 Dimensional Swans, and it will get posted. Here's the schedule:

August - Hiatus

September -> November: (Manifest)
Sep 7: Gimme Shelter
Sep 14: Slips and Tangles
Sep 21: You Are a Light
Sep 28: End of the World
Oct 5: End of the World (Part 2)
Oct 7: Forward March
Oct 12: Harness Your Hopes
Oct 19: Space Oddity
Oct 26: Bitter Divisions
Nov 7: Epilogue - Mt. Washington

Dec 7: Everything Must Go!

As I said on the main page, this is definitely hopeful, but I think it's realistic. The only thing that might change is the chapter titles. And I'm a bit iffy on there being an epilogue at all, but I want something to come out on the seventh, and there's only one character who it should be about.
(Manifest) is sort of the beginner's intro to 3DS, as it starts when the going really gets good, and follows a lot of the major historical developments in the world. If you've read Tubular Logic or any of the other stories set in this world, then that's kind of hard mode... so props to you!
I might also host it on other sites, just in an attempt to get eyes on it, since I'm absolutely horrid at advertising myself. We'll see.
So yeah, chapters every Monday, with bonuses on the seventh of each month. You'll see why when Slips and Tangles comes out. I really want to do something every seventh for the forseeable future, but I don't think it's too likely, especially when I'm deep in the novel-writing trenches.
Oh yeah! I'm gearing up to write the next novel! And the next entry in 3DS! I got some advice from an artist I really admire and it gave me the hope to revitalise my old ideas. Well, a lot of it is getting thrown out of the window for complicated reasons, but I'm ready to buckle down and get some brainstorming/outlining done. But, I promised myself I'd finish (Manifest) first, and I want something for December, so I'm going to try to finish up Everything Must Go! It's possible I'll pivot to something else, but not super likely, I don't think.
I really wanna write more 3DS, since it's sort of my magnum opus, and I already have a whole novel languishing in my drafts. And ideas for like, a lot more. I'll definitely provide some more background info on what 3DS really is at some point (cos the current page is kinda meh imo), and maybe I'll add it to this post, or I'll make a new one. But right now, I don't think that's super important. What is important, is that you're going to get at least one new chapter every week for two whole months! Consistency!
I've said it before, and I'll say it again, but I love serialised art. I want people to come back every week for something new. And it's going to happen. Check back on September seventh.
