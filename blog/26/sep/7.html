sep 7, 2026 - manifest
This was a hard post to write! And it's long! There's a TL;DR after the divider.
So. I really wanted to have the first draft of (Manifest) finished by now. But due to writer's block killing a week of my time, the focus of certain chapters shifting, the battle against scope creep, and, of course, being insanely busy, I'm still only done with seven out of a planned ten chapters.[1] Oh yeah, and there was that two week span where I thought I was going to have to move at the drop of a dime and had to spend all my free time apartment hunting. Sigh.
It's very easy to just say "Eh, fuck it, post what you got! That's a month of lead time!" But unfortunately that's a little difficult for me, for many reasons. The first is that I'm still figuring this out. If you recall, I've written a whole novel in this world, and that novel had a lot of odds and ends that I wasn't entirely sure of, but knew worked thematically. Writing out the history of this world for (Manifest) led me to a lot of revelations that tied up some of those ideas in a neater thematic package. So, anyone who's read the book (a few of my friends), has read a version that's incomplete, in my eyes. That's to say: the more I write, the more I understand this world. Ideally, I'd have everything written before publishing. But it's a little hard to do that, especially when you're aiming for upwards of a million words. So I do want to serialise it and post reasonably sized chunks when they're done. It's just the best strategy.
But, then the siren's song of not having to care about that, and just publishing my shitty first draft definitely calls. It would be so easy to say fuck it and just not care. If it's bad, it's bad, and that's fine. But this is my baby, my masterpiece, and I can't stomach the thought of the beginning, the part that's really supposed to sell people, being bad. I'm shivering imagining people saying "just skip to the trolls", you know? (Not that I think early Homestuck is bad by any stretch.) It's just that I want it all to be good, especially since the beginning is quite important! There's some stuff set up in chapter one which probably won't get explored for novels and novels worth of wordcount.
Of course, there's always the route of doing a hard edit once I'm finished with everything a decade or two down the line. I'm not opposed to it, but that'd be a lot of time and effort for a story that's done and over. I'm planning a complete rewrite for my novel, and that's really frustrating! I've already done several hard editing passes and now I'm rewriting the whole thing? Alas, it is necessary. It was the first time I actually wrote a story longer than a few pages[3], and it's pretty rough, especially in the beginning.
Another reason for the delay is a fun thematic one. The first chapter takes place on February 7, 1950, and the same day, 50 years later. Do I want to delay all the way until February? Five whole months? Maybe![4] And, of course, with five more months of lead time, that means I can do so much! It's taken me... far too long to write (Manifest). I began maybe a few days into July, and I've gotten close to 30k in the two months since then.[5] And so waiting until February means that I'm going to be able to get a lot done. So, let's play in this space.
What would a theoretical February release schedule look like? The seventh falls on a Sunday, and given I want to release chapters on Mondays, I could either skip or publish on the eighth. I'm leaning towards the latter, for a multitude of reasons.[6] Weekly chapters every Monday until March, when there's the same situation.[7] I'm unsure, but I'll say skip. Skipping April 7th as well means that the final chapter of (Manifest) will publish on April 12, 2027. Not bad. Even factoring in editing, I'm almost certain that I'll have finished EMG! by then, and will probably be knuckle deep in PARACOSM rewrites. Maybe I'll even have started outlining for the PARACOSM sequel![8] (Probably not though, because I'm going to be genuinely shocked if I write a single word in December.) I've had the idea for that book for too long and if I'm not working on it by this time next year, I am going to be very very sad.
Anyway, my point is that if I'm lucky, and I delay until February, you might get (Manifest) and EMG! back to back. How fun would that be? I've written the beginning of that story, and I'm unsure how I would release it. It's structured like Tubular Logic, with scenes/segments sometimes only a paragraph or two in length, and sometimes longer. I don't think I'm going to want to publish EMG! all at once like I did TL, considering that in my notes, it's going to be even longer than (Manifest), but I'll definitely have a clearer picture of that once I'm more than a few thousand words into it.[10] I don't think it'll end up ballooning like (M) did, but only time will tell.
The only real downside, I guess, to publishing in February, (besides breaking my promise and also denying my desire to share this story with other people), is that Mt. Washington doesn't have a good place to land. I had planned it that way since it takes place between (M) and EMG, and it's very fitting for it to be released on November seventh. But it doesn't seem like that's going to happen now. Hey, maybe I'll finish (M) and Mt. Washington by then, and I'll release it as a fun treat to tide you over while you're waiting for February to roll around. More likely, I think, is it coming out on Nov.7, 2027. Whether that's before or after EMG, I'm unsure, but I have more than a year to think about it.
Oh goodness me, I just realised that if I sit on all of (M) and EMG, then with PARACOSM (and its rewrite being even longer) at 100k words, then I'll be sitting on top of over 200k of unpublished material. Yipes! It might not feel like a lot, especially if you often read doorstopper fantasy like I do, but if you've ever written 200k words, you know it's a kind of dizzying amount of work to have behind you. At least I have all that time to really punch up everything and make it consistent mechanically and thematically.


Yeesh, that was a lot. Is that everything regarding 3DS and my current projects? I believe so.
TL;DR: (Manifest) is getting delayed to February, where you'll get weekly chapters into April. Final word count will be around 45k. By then I hope to have finished Everything Must Go!, which will be of similar length. Unsure of its publishing date. Then the rewrite of my novel, PARACOSM, then I'll start work on its sequel. Maybe swap the last two.
I'm also going to write an update blog post like this (hopefully shorter) on the seventh of every month. (Are you sensing a theme?) Mostly just progress updates, thoughts on the schedule and maybe snippets of other projects I'm trying to juggle.

On that note, I've been pretty active in the Inchworm discord server. I wrote a fun analysis of Danny and his relationship with Taylor, although as someone on tumblr pointed out, I did miss a pretty crucial scene (oops!). I've also been doing... not a liveblog. Liveanalysis? Of the novel Ra by qntm. If you're in there, feel free to say hi. If you're not, please give a listen to one of my favourite podcasts! Some listeners haven't read Worm, which is a little strange but understandable, so don't feel like you have to read Worm to understand the podcast. Check me out in the episode length guess thread - I haven't won yet, but my time will come. I'm also now addicted to Outlier because of them. (Thread is in off-topic-general.) The server may seem a little big, but there's not an overabundance of active users. As someone who has never ever been active in a server not consisting of half a dozen of IRL friends, they're very nice and welcoming.
And as someone who has never been active in big discords, I hate restricting any of my writing to a discord, let alone restricting my Ra analysis to something as niche as a discord for a podcast about a book which is tangentially similar to Ra. If I ever go back to correct that error in the Danny essay, It'll definitely go up here. I'm unsure how I would port my thoughts on Ra, to be honest, and it might take some time to clean them up, but maybe when I'm finished, they'll all go on a page here somewhere.

Christ I'm tired. This has taken hours to write and was quite emotionally draining. I still haven't even transcribed everything I've written yet... I hope you know it breaks my heart to have to delay publication like this. I just want to share this world and these people with you. But it'll probably make for a better story in the long run. I said before and I'll say it again: 3DS is my baby. And I'm not good at improvising. You'll see it eventually, I swear. (That being said, I really do need beta readers, and if you've made it this far, you probably care, so feel free to shoot me an email or DM on bluesky. You'll get these stories months before the public does.)
What else..? Oh, I'm working on an illustration for Tubular Logic. A friend of mine works at a print shop and I was talking to him about getting some little booklets of the story printed, and I'd rather have a nice detailed illustration over the MS Paint tier scribbles I did for the opengraph thumbnail. It's mostly finished, but the last few bits are stubborn. It'll get done, though.
You also would've seen some previews if you're in the Inchworm server, but I'm working (very very slowly) on a Worm PMV. I don't expect this one to get done, and progress has already stalled, but it'd be nice if I did finish, one day.
I think that's all I have to say for now. Thanks so much for your patience.

-J
