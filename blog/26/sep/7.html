sep 7, 2026 - manifest
This is a hard post to write! I really wanted to have the first draft of (Manifest) finished by now. But due to writer's block killing a week of my time, the focus of certain chapters shifting, the battle against scope creep, and, of course, being insanely busy, I'm still only done with seven out of a planned ten chapters.[1]
It's very easy to just say "Eh, fuck it, post what you got! That's a month of lead time!" But unfortunately that's a little difficult for me, for many reasons. The first is that I'm still figuring this out. If you recall, I've written a whole novel in this world, and that novel had a lot of odds and ends that I wasn't entirely sure of, but knew worked thematically. Writing out the history of this world led me to a lot of revelations that tied up some of those ideas in a neater thematic package. So, anyone who's read the book (a few of my friends), have read a version that's incomplete, in my eyes. That's to say: the more I write, the more I understand this world. Ideally, I'd have everything written before publishing. But it's a little hard to do that, especially when you're aiming for upwards of a million words. So I do want to serialise it and post reasonably sized chunks when they're done.
But, then the siren's song of not having to care about that, and just publishing my shitty first draft. It would be so easy to say fuck it and just not care. If it's bad, it's bad, and that's fine. But this is my baby, my masterpiece, and I can't stomach the thought of the beginning, the part that's really supposed to sell people, being bad. I'm shivering imagining people saying "just skip to the trolls", you know? I want it all to be good, especially since the beginning is quite important! There's some stuff set up in chapter one which probably won't get explored for novels and novels worth of wordcount.
Of course, there's always the route of doing a hard edit once I'm finished. I'm not opposed to it, but that'd be a lot of time and effort for a story that's already completed. I'm already planning a complete rewrite for the novel I wrote, and that sucks! I've already done several hard editing passes and now I'm rewriting the whole thing? Alas, it is necessary. It was the first time I actually wrote anything more than a few pages in one world[3], and it's pretty rough, especially in the beginning.
Another reason for the delay is a fun thematic one. The first chapter takes place on February 7, 1950, and the same day, 50 years later. Do I want to delay all the way until February? Five whole months? Maybe![4] And, of course, with five more months of lead time, that means I can do so much! It's taken me... far too long to write (Manifest). I began maybe a few days into July, and I've gotten close to 30k in the two months since then.[5] And so waiting until February means that I'm going to be able to get a lot done. Let's play in this space.
So, what would a theoretical February release schedule look like? The seventh falls on a Sunday, and given I want to release chapters on Mondays, I could either skip or publish on the eighth. I'm leaning towards the latter, for a multitude of reasons.[6] Weekly chapters every Monday until March, when there's the same situation.[7] I'm unsure, but I'll say skip. Skipping April 7th as well means that the final chapter of (Manifest) will publish on April 12, 2027. Not bad. Even factoring in editing, I'm almost certain that I'll have finished EMG! by then, and will probably be knuckle deep in PARACOSM rewrites. Maybe I'll even have started outlining for the PARACOSM sequel![8]

Peace.
