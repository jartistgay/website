jun 6, 2026 - archive progress and six corners
Progress is going smoothly on the archive. Well, as smooth as it gets when you have ADHD. Stopping and starting, you know the drill. It's quite fun though, and I feel I've got the hang of it. Learning HTML/CSS at the same time has also been interesting. Well, learning the things I need to learn in order to get everything looking the way I want it to. Definitely helps that the creator of this template made everything pretty neat and tidy.
You can tell I got indents working. Turns out, it wasn't as hard as I thought it would be, although I did use a slightly goofy workaround. I'm unsure why learning all this on the fly seems so much easier than it used to be. Maybe that's the difference being medicated makes, I don't know.

Anyways, I've been slowly writing again. Fingers crossed I'm sticking with it. This is a story I've dreamed up for a while called Six Corners (very tentative title lol). It's sort of cyberpunk thriller, though if you know one thing about my storytelling proclivities, it's that I'm genre agnostic; tone is really what drives me as an artist (in any medium, really).
My fear though, is that the issues that plagued my second novel (The Saints), will also plague this one. Namely: a lack of planning. My first book was so meticulously planned because I had been thinking about that world and its characters for the better part of a decade. (The inception of the main characters actually dates back to early 2013 when I saw a very very bad anime that didn't deliver on its promises. But that's a story for another time). The problem is that I'm not much of a planner as a writer, and that's what doomed every writing project I had before I wrote Paracosm. But I can't just sit on Six Corners for a decade before I actually write it. For reasons I should hope are obvious.
So, what do? I did a lot of writing out my problems and ways to combat them (writing is just how I think best, big surprise), but you don't really need to be subjected to that (unless you want to?) so I've cut all that out. But all of that made me far more confident in Six Corners than I used to be, so that's quite nice. I also have some solid opportunities for the next month to really focus on this, so I'm hoping to have part 1/3 finished by then. Maybe wishful thinking, but we'll see.
I also started drafting up some demo pages for what the formatting would look like if I hosted Six Corners on this website, just as a fun exercise, and it made me even more excited for this project. Except I've only just started the second chapter, so I'm going to disappear into my writing hole before I put anything out there. I hope to at least have something by the end of the month; expect any updates for this project around then.

Peace.
