oct 7, 2026 - monthly update
Getting started on this one early (Sep 19), because I think it would be nice if I wrote this as the month went along, rather than all at once. So, I've hit a wall with (Manifest). Glad I decided to put it on hold. I also had a way better idea for the opening chapter, so I'm glad I didn't put it up on the seventh when I wanted to. The wall I hit mostly has to do with compressing like a decade's worth of time into one chapter. I probably would have figured something out, but I'm currently losing my mind with IRL matters, and have had no time to write. And when I do have the time, I'm just drained. I'm trying to work past that block, but it's difficult. I think cataloguing my thoughts will help, though.
Another thing is that I've been putting my writing through TTS and listening to it. Something about the layer of abstraction really helps me get out of my own head about it, and (I think) more easily identify when something's not working. Good news there is that I'm probably not going to rewrite all of PARACOSM. A lot of it really does work, and I'm almost surprised at how well it holds up. My main issues are the opening chapters, and the PoV chapters from people who are not the main two. Oh, and everything needs a lot more space to breathe, I think. Same issue here, in that I tried to squeeze so much time into a small wordcount.
I'm also quite excited to add to PARACOSM. After not having read it for 6+ months, and the added layer of TTS, it's pretty clear, I think, what's missing, and boy I'm excited to revisit these characters I love.
The TTS has also impressed upon me how much I really want to produce an audio version of 3DS. I really love audiobooks, and think they're an important accessibility tool. Of course, I have absolutely no experience making audiobooks, but I spent years GMing TTRPGs, so I think my ability to do voices and carry narration is pretty good. It'll also keep me from going back changing the story too heavily, since messing with the audio would be annoying. I might do a proof of concept for this idea, recording an audio version of Tamsyn Muir's The Unwanted Guest, cos I've been itching for that since the day it came out.

It's now the 28th and I have more thoughts!
First, I still want the audio versions of my stories. And I really wanna do Tubular Logic. It's maybe the work I'm proudest of right now, and I think it'll work better through audio than 25 would.
The big thing, here, I think, is that I'm no longer sure if (Manifest) is the best place to start 3DS. I voiced this concern in last month's update, but I want to put my best foot forwards. Not that getting readers is the ultimate goal here (artistic fulfillment is), but it would be nice to have readers, and obviously the better it is, the more satisfied I'll be with it!
One of the things that I struggled with when showing PARACOSM to other people was just sort of the breadth of history and worldbuilding that I just... failed to communicate. That's one of my biggest failures as a writer, and I'm totally at fault for it. I've long joked that I'm allergic to exposition, but that's led me to shy away from any exposition, instead of trying my hand at writing more subtle exposition.
So, of course, instead of learning to just do that... I wrote a whole forty-some-odd thousand words of backstory. (Not that I regret it, I'll get to it later.) One of the things I've been worrying about the most with regards to (Manifest) is if it's even the best place to start. Is it even that interesting as a story, instead of just being lore? Further, is it interesting when I compress so much time and history into such a small wordcount? Or does that strip the fun out of it?
These questions are not without answers, of course. An idea I've been toying with (maybe very stupid?) is just giving you all the first chapter of every good, solid place to begin 3DS, and letting you decide. Problem with that is that I don't have very many readers, I don't think. Hard to get exact numbers, but... yeah. Honestly, even one vote would be enough, to know that I'm playing to an audience. Those chapters, by the way, would be (Manifest), PARACOSM, Everything Must Go!, and a fourth project I haven't talked much about, called Terminal.
And, for the record, I'm not mad or disappointed or anything that I've spent so much time on (Manifest) (and to a lesser extent, EMG). I believe I said it last month, but there's so much that I've been able to figure out in the process of writing that puts my previous thoughts and writings in much sharper focus.
In that vein, I've also been doing some serious thinking about PARACOSM. There's definitely a lot that needs to be changed. One of the things I think I failed at, besides the exposition, is the worldbuilding. It's there, for sure, but it's not nearly as dense as I'd like. Today I picked up the audiobook for The Traitor Baru Cormorant, (which I've read before, but not listened to), and it was really illuminating. Of course, I'll never be Seth Dickinson, or Ann Leckie, or Martha Wells, or Tamsyn Muir[1], but I'll at least hope for an Orson Scott Card. The worldbuilding notes I jotted down are a little scary, but they're fun and interesting. Also fun note! More than half of PARACOSM's main cast now has different names for the first time in like, seven or eight years. It's been really hard to switch my perceptions of them.

-J
[1] I tried not to footnote but also I remember Muir saying in an interview that she doesn't worldbuild for its own sake and all her worldbuilding has a purpose and it made me want to tear at the walls! I worldbuild for its own sake and I'm still not doing half of what she's done! Grr. This is definitely something I've been trying to come to terms with (especially while reading Ra), but I need to remind myself that the reason I adore the artists I do is that they are able to do things that I cannot. And the obverse of that being that people will enjoy my art, even if I'm not good at those things. Because there are things I am good at that they are not.
