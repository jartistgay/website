oct 10, 2026 - monthly update
Getting started on this one early (Sep 19), because I think it would be nice if I wrote this as the month went along, rather than all at once. So, I've hit a wall with (Manifest). Glad I decided to put it on hold. I also had a way better idea for the opening chapter, so I'm glad I didn't put it up on the seventh when I wanted to. The wall I hit mostly has to do with compressing like a decade's worth of time into one chapter. I probably would have figured something out, but I'm currently losing my mind with IRL matters, and have had no time to write. And when I do have the time, I'm just drained. I'm trying to work past that block, but it's difficult. I think cataloguing my thoughts will help, though.
Another thing is that I've been putting my writing through TTS and listening to it. Something about the layer of abstraction really helps me get out of my own head about it, and (I think) more easily identify when something's not working. Good news there is that I'm probably not going to rewrite all of PARACOSM. A lot of it really does work, and I'm almost surprised at how well it holds up. My main issues are the opening chapters, and the PoV chapters from people who are not the main two. Oh, and everything needs a lot more space to breathe, I think. Same issue here, in that I tried to squeeze so much time into a small wordcount.
I'm also quite excited to add to PARACOSM. After not having read it for 6+ months, and the added layer of TTS, it's pretty clear, I think, what's missing, and boy I'm excited to revisit these characters I love.
The TTS has also impressed upon me how much I really want to produce an audio version of 3DS. I really love audiobooks, and think they're an important accessibility tool. Of course, I have absolutely no experience making audiobooks, but I spent years GMing TTRPGs, so I think my ability to do voices and carry narration is pretty good. It'll also keep me from going back changing the story too heavily, since messing with the audio would be annoying. I might do a proof of concept for this idea, recording an audio version of Tamsyn Muir's The Unwanted Guest, cos I've been itching for that since the day it came out.

It's now the 28th and I have more thoughts!
First, I still want the audio versions of my stories. And I really wanna do Tubular Logic. It's maybe the work I'm proudest of right now, and I think it'll work better through audio than 25 would.
The big thing, here, I think, is that I'm no longer sure if (Manifest) is the best place to start 3DS. I voiced this concern in last month's update, but I want to put my best foot forwards. Not that getting readers is the ultimate goal here (artistic fulfillment is), but it would be nice to have readers, and obviously the better it is, the more satisfied I'll be with it!
One of the things that I struggled with when showing PARACOSM to other people was just sort of the breadth of history and worldbuilding that I just... failed to communicate. That's one of my biggest failures as a writer, and I'm totally at fault for it. I've long joked that I'm allergic to exposition, but that's led me to shy away from any exposition, instead of trying my hand at writing more subtle exposition.
So, of course, instead of learning to just do that... I wrote a whole forty-some-odd thousand words of backstory. (Not that I regret it, I'll get to it later.) One of the things I've been worrying about the most with regards to (Manifest) is if it's even the best place to start. Is it even that interesting as a story, instead of just being lore? Further, is it interesting when I compress so much time and history into such a small wordcount? Or does that strip the fun out of it?
And, for the record, I'm not mad or disappointed or anything that I've spent so much time on (Manifest) (and to a lesser extent, EMG). I believe I said it last month, but there's so much that I've been able to figure out in the process of writing that puts my previous thoughts and writings in much sharper focus.
In that vein, I've also been doing some serious thinking about PARACOSM. There's definitely a lot that needs to be changed. One of the things I think I failed at, besides the exposition, is the worldbuilding. It's there, for sure, but it's not nearly as dense as I'd like. Today I picked up the audiobook for The Traitor Baru Cormorant, (which I've read before, but not listened to), and it was really illuminating. Of course, I'll never be Seth Dickinson, or Ann Leckie, or Martha Wells, or Tamsyn Muir[1], but I'll at least hope for an Orson Scott Card. The worldbuilding notes I jotted down are a little scary, but they're fun and interesting. Also fun note! More than half of PARACOSM's main cast now has different names for the first time in like, seven or eight years. It's been really hard to switch my perceptions of them.

It's now the third and I have more thoughts!
So first off, I got injured at work. That definitely sucked in the moment (it was bad enough to require stitches), but I'm not going to lie, the time off has been a godsend. As I'm writing this, it's been less than a week, and the amount of work that I've gotten done is kind of staggering. (Somewhere around 10k over five days? Plus a lot of work on organisation and transcribing.) I haven't been this productive since I started working on 3DS all the way back in March of last year. And if you're reading this when it comes out, then I still have at least a few more days off. Yippee!
So, most of the progress has been made on Everything Must Go!. It's a fun story, and one I really want to tell. It's not exactly turning out how I expected it to, which isn't bad, necessarily, just interesting. I've also kind of given up on EMG! as a good intro point for 3DS. Well, 'given up' sounds bad; that's not the case. It is kind of a good intro for certain aspects, and I kind of think that weaving it together with (Manifest) is the way to go? (M) is a lot longer, so I think it might be fun to have chapters of EMG! as sort of interstitials to tide people over. Each segment of it is around 400-500 words in length, and there's going to be around 20 in total. So maybe two for every chapter of (M)? Sounds about right.
I'm still kind of blocked WRT (Manifest), and it's the same problem I was stuck on before, condensing a lot of time into very few words. If I had played with narration in the opposite way, it would be very easy to wave a hand and say, 'oh, well, then fifty years passed and here's two sentences that summarise what happen and what our characters took from it'. But (Manifest) doesn't have a narrator like that, in fact, the narration does not let itself be known and frequently drops away to pure dialogue. The contrast there would be... not amazing, I don't think? I'm kind of unsure. I'd need some readers to let me know for sure, and I don't have those :L
I did draft up a bit of diegetic narration as a weird kind of intro to (M), a chapter 0 so to speak, and while I liked it, it's not like it made everything click, in the way that it did when I first had the idea. That probably means I botched something in the execution, but I don't know what, and if I don't know that, I can't fix it. Sigh.
Anyway, hashed this one out way quicker than I did the others, and I'd like to keep it that way. Maybe one more update before the seventh, but there'll be a progress report on the seventh, so I hope you enjoy that!

Ha, whoops! Didn't mean to forget about this update for the last few days. I've just been busy with wrapping up EMG! and personal life stuff (just bought my dream car today lol). So, EMG! is getting split up. I was flying by the seat of my pants for the first half (Chapters 0-4), but the back half (5-9) really solidified into something I'm very very proud of. Chapters 10-23 are going to be published after PARACOSM. At least, if I don't change my mind about it.
This is mostly because my idea for those chapters was essentially the leadup into the PARACOSM sequel, and I feel a little bad writing all that leadup only to leave those characters up in the air for several years. So now they end in a place that's a little happier, and works better as an ending to its own story. Also, writing 20k words in less than two weeks is genuinely draining, and I don't think I can keep this up, at least not with those characters. They've reached a good stopping point.
Also, can you believe I wrote so much in so little time? I started on the 30th and wrote every day, finishing the story up today. I already had some stuff written out, and it's technically only like 19.6k, but that's close to 2k every day. Maybe doesn't seem like a ton to most people, but it really adds up. And on top of that, most of it was handwritten, too. Turns out that rolling up to a coffee shop with a notebook and a pen really does it for me. Of course, I kind of waste time transcribing everything, but that's more like a first editing pass than anything, and, of course, it wouldn't be done at all if it wasn't handwritten. So it's not a waste of time, when you look at it that way.
EMG definitely needs some editing before it's public, and I'm still holding out hope that it and (Manifest) will be ready by February. But it seems somewhat realistic, I think! Now that I know how well this works for me, I might spend a lot more time writing, even after I go back to work. Speaking of, I'm probably going to have to return to work on Wendesday (the 14th) so that means three more days of intensive writing. I really really want to finish up the first draft of (M), but I do acknowledge that I may be still blocked there. I began a draft for a chapter, but I wasn't a huge fan of it. The good thing about the coffee shop method, though, is that (at least with EMG), writer's block kind of can't stand up to just my sheer desire to write! I'm sure I'll find a way to write it so that I'm satisfied.
I didn't expect to get as attached to the EMG characters as I ended up being. They were kind of a dumb throwaway idea I toyed with before I really buckled down on (Manifest), funnily enough. But like I said, I'm quite proud of what I've done here. Those characters share a culture with the setting of PARACOSM, and I had a lot of fun embellishing certain aspects - I said before that the main characters of PARACOSM now have new names, and that's a consequence of those embellishments. It'll be so fun to go back and bring these stories more in lockstep with each other.
Actually, I just realised how much this has come to roost! The plotline I ended up going with in EMG (stranded in a desert) was a plotline I originally intended for the main characters of PARACOSM, and even before that, a plotline I intended for "Z", which was the first inklings of this story that dripped out of my brain sometime in 2017. In the magic system for that world, some people randomly receive a simple magical ability when they become an adult. The main character was a girl with the ability to create water, and I had a whole novel-length plotline for her, where her ship crashes in a desert, and she has to use her limited water generation to try to keep her allies alive. Basically nothing of that initial conception really carried over, but I could see that desert storyline so clear in my mind. I'm glad to have finally put it to paper.
That's all from me, for now. I'll try to get an update on time next month, but knowing me and knowing what will be happening in my life, I promise nothing! I'm so glad to have finished another leg of this story, even if it's a shorter one. And thanks for reading! Until next time,

-J
