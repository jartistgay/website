may 23, 2026 - thoughts on transness and community
(If you see this early, no you don't! This probably won't even get finished until the 24th, since I'm helping a friend move today, and that will likely only add to the number of thoughts I have on this subject.) Just had an amazing day with a group of trans people and it reminded me of how much I missed the capital C trans Community. For context, I don't necessarily consider myself trans. Gender is a social construct etc etc, but also, transness is a category that people invented to describe certain attitudes I hold towards my body and certain ways I prefer to dress. It's not something I innately... am.

I dunno, maybe I'm just getting lost i the weeds here, as this is an idea I've been trying to articulate for a long while now, and have never quite succeeded. Just as "female" and "male" are categories assigned to us at birth, "trans", and 'cis' are categories assigned to us because of actions we do or do not take.
