Alma:
I really like the application of time jumps here, you get to provide good backstory without long exposition, and it isn't too clingy to the backstory either, if that makes sense.

J:
I thought about this a lot, mostly because I'm a big fan of progression fantasy, but I absolutely do not have the patience to write progression fantasy. So this was a fun way to get a little close to that, with the time skips.
Another reason was that I thought people would just get bored if I covered every little detail. I know I would.
Also, I did not forsee this story being as long as it ended up. I kind of expected this to be around the 80% mark, but that didn't turn out to be the case...
